Mission Context

Problem Statement & The Core Engineering Challenge

Overcoming extreme space communication latency while engineering a cost-effective, versatile exploration platform.
Planetary Exploration Realities
Extraterrestrial exploration operates under severe environmental constraints that prevent continuous Earth-side control:
  • Latency & Blackouts: 4 to 24-minute round-trip delay prohibits manual joystick teleoperation; onboard autonomy is mandatory.
  • Severe Wheel Slippage: Fine sand and scree cause catastrophic odometric drift and immobilization.
  • Subsurface Science Necessity: Surface regolith is degraded by UV and cosmic radiation, requiring core drilling to reach preserved geological layers.
The Cost-Effective Multi-Purpose Challenge
Conventional exploration rovers rely on bespoke, multimillion-dollar aerospace components, limiting accessibility and modularity:
  • Accessible Multi-Mission Platform: Delivering a modular rover capable of scouting, subsurface core sampling, dexterous manipulation, and wet-lab analysis on a cost-effective budget.
  • Industrial Reliability: Leveraging commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) high-torque hub motors and industrial microcontrollers to achieve aerospace-grade durability.
  • Seamless Sim2Real Transition: Validating full navigation and kinematic dynamics in Gazebo physics before physical commissioning.

Integrated Solution

Proposed Solution: The Unified Rover System

A multi-capability architecture harnessing the combined power of modern edge AI, distributed MCUs, and direct-drive actuation.
The Autonomous Stack
High-level spatial awareness and path intelligence running on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano:
  • 3D Perception: Voxel filtering, RANSAC ground-plane extraction, and DBSCAN obstacle clustering.
  • Slip-Adaptive EKF & SLAM: 15-state dynamic covariance tuning with RTAB-Map graph loop closures.
  • Nav2 Intelligence: Smac Hybrid A* global paths and MPPI local predictive trajectory optimization.
Multi-ECU STM32 System
Distributed real-time microcontrollers partitioned into deterministic domains:
  • Domain Isolation: Dedicated STMs for Locomotion PID, Drill & Tool Feed, and Science Sensor acquisition.
  • Robust Fault Containment: Bus-isolated architecture ensuring payload errors never compromise vehicle mobility.
  • Real-Time Determinism: Hardware timer quadrature decoding, active stall protection, and E-Stop loops.
High-Torque Hub Motors
In-wheel direct-drive technology built for extreme planetary tractive demands:
  • Zero Transmission Backlash: Direct wheel integration eliminates fragile chains, belts, and external gearboxes.
  • High Climbing Tractive Force: Massive low-speed stall torque easily negotiates steep slopes, scree, and loose sand.
  • Independent Wheel Control: Precise per-wheel speed modulation for agile skid-steer zero-radius turning.

Phase 1 Milestone

Deliverables Phase 1: Rover Platform & Core Drill

Establishing complete mobility, autonomous traversal, and mechanical subsurface sample drilling.
Core Deliverable 1.1 & 1.2
Autonomous Hub Motor Rover Base
A fully realized 4-wheel direct-drive mobile chassis capable of traversing rugged terrain under full onboard ROS 2 autonomy:
  • Direct-Drive Hub Powertrain: 4× High-torque in-wheel hub motors with central PDB regulation.
  • Multi-ECU Architecture: Dedicated STM32 MCUs handling closed-loop PID velocity control and safety interlocks.
  • State Estimation Suite: 15-state EKF with adaptive slip rejection and RTAB-Map graph SLAM.
  • Autonomous Navigation: Nav2 Smac Hybrid A* path planner and MPPI predictive trajectory tracker.
  • Perception & Markers: 3D obstacle cluster publisher and ArUco marker pose estimator.
Core Deliverable 1.3
Subsurface Core Drill Mechanism
An active electromechanical sampling assembly designed to extract intact regolith cores from beneath the surface:
  • Dual-Motor Drive: Independent high-speed rotational spindle and precision linear feed lead screw.
  • Automated Deployment: Servo-actuated deployment mechanism for rigid ground engagement.
  • Dynamic Stall Protection: Real-time current sensing to automatically modulate penetration speed based on soil resistance.
  • State Machine Automation: Single-command execution: Deploy → Drill → Sample → Retract.

Phase 2 Milestone

Deliverables Phase 2: Robotic Arm & In-Situ Science Kit

Expanding the rover's capabilities with dexterous manipulation and onboard geochemical analysis.
Core Deliverable 2.1
Articulated Robotic Manipulator
A multi-axis dexterous robotic arm capable of retrieving, handling, and manipulating samples and field equipment:
  • Multi-DoF Actuation: 4× NEMA 17 steppers (closed-loop servo controller boards) + 2× high-torque DC joints.
  • Motion Planning: MoveIt 2 integration for collision-free trajectory execution and inverse kinematics.
  • Tactile Gripper: Force-sensitive resistor (FSR) end-effector providing tactile slip-free feedback.
  • Sample Handling: Autonomous extraction of sample containers and placement into science bay.
Core Deliverable 2.2 & 2.3
In-Situ Science Analysis Suite & Trials
An automated geochemical laboratory performing real-time analysis on freshly drilled regolith cores:
  • Gravimetric Measurement: 3-point load cell array with HX711 24-bit digitizers for high-precision mass calculation.
  • Electrochemical Profiling: Soil pH probe and signal conditioner assessing acidity/alkalinity.
  • Automated Carousel: Servo-driven sealed chamber preventing atmospheric contamination.
  • End-to-End Mission Proof: Autonomous navigate → drill → transfer → analyze → publish telemetry.
